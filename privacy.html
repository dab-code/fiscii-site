Fiscii privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Fiscii is a small fishing game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nobody (including the developer) receives your data, and nothing is sold.

Location and weather

If you allow it, Fiscii reads your approximate (coarse) location while the app is open, so the pond can show your real local weather. It never asks for precise or background location.

  • The position is rounded to about 11 km (0.1°) on your phone before it is used.
  • That rounded position is sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), a free weather service, to fetch the current weather. No account, key or identifier is sent with it. Like any web server, Open-Meteo sees your IP address and may keep request logs (which can include the rounded coordinates) for up to 90 days; see its privacy section.
  • The same rounded position is passed to Android's built-in Geocoder to show a place name (for example your town) next to the weather. On most phones the Geocoder is provided by Google Play services and may look the name up online under Google's privacy policy.
  • The rounded position, the place name and the last weather reading are kept only on your phone.

If you deny location, the game still works with calm default weather.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification, scheduled on your phone. No push service or server is involved. It is off until you turn it on.

Your save

Your progress (fish, coins, settings) is stored on your phone only. If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings, not by Fiscii. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the save.

Children

Fiscii collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
